EAST CHEM PLC — favicon / Google search icon fix
==================================================

Why Google shows a globe icon instead of your logo:
Google reads the icon from a `favicon.ico` file at your site's root
first, and your repo never had one — only `favicon.png`. Missing
`favicon.ico` is the most common reason Google falls back to a
generic globe (sometimes shown as the hosting provider's icon).

REPLACE these 5 files (same names, root folder):
  - index.html
  - about.html
  - contact.html
  - products.html
  - admin.html
(each now declares favicon.ico first, then favicon.png, then an
apple-touch-icon — all pointing at your existing logo icon)

ADD this new file:
  - favicon.ico   (a proper multi-size icon file generated from your
                    existing favicon.png — nothing to design, just
                    upload it)

After uploading, wait ~1 minute for Vercel to redeploy, then:
  1. Visit https://east-chem-plc.vercel.app/favicon.ico directly —
     you should see the small logo icon load (not a 404).
  2. In Google Search Console (Settings you already set up), open
     URL Inspection, enter your homepage URL, and click
     "Request Indexing" again — this nudges Google to recrawl and
     pick up the new icon sooner.

One thing to know: even after this, Google can take anywhere from a
few days to a few weeks to actually refresh the icon shown in search
results — there's no way to force it instantly, and repeating
"Request Indexing" many times in a row does not speed it up further.
